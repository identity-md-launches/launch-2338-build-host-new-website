# Asset provenance

- The visual tokens and layout rules in `src/brand-tokens.css` come from [VIMD Family v3](https://github.com/blockmerchantlabs/vimd-family/tree/v3), with its [visual identity](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/blockmerchantlabs/vimd-family/v3/visualidentity.md) as the brief’s source of truth. The site credits the package in its footer.
- `public/assets/investor-pepe.png` is the supplied [example investor Pepe](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/blockmerchantlabs/vimd-family/v3/assets/example-investor-pepe.png), preserved unchanged, used as logo, portrait, favicon and share image. The package attributes this variant to [IMD image job de4618d8-66ce-42b2-ba9b-2f13a8e88ce9](https://explorer.imd.fun/jobs/de4618d8-66ce-42b2-ba9b-2f13a8e88ce9); Pepe the Frog is Matt Furie’s character. The package’s blanket token/text permission does not cover its images.
- IBM Plex Mono Latin WOFF2 files, regular 400/500/600, are bundled locally from Fontsource’s IBM Plex Mono distribution. The IBM font license is at `public/assets/OFL.txt` and is retained in the export.
- MetaMask, Rabby and Phantom SVG wallet marks were copied from RainbowKit’s official `packages/rainbowkit/src/wallets/walletConnectors/` assets. The Coinbase mark follows the SVG in the supplied wallet connector reference. These identify wallet products, with their original colors. RainbowKit’s MIT license is retained in `licenses/rainbowkit-MIT.txt`.
- `browser-wallet.svg` is a small code-native wallet outline drawn for this site using the supplied slate secondary-text color.
- Better Interface design guidance is adapted from Jakub Krehel (MIT), commit `267330e1adfc66a718fb65fa6918c1f06d0a689e`. The design-documentation method is adapted from Paul Bakaus’s Impeccable (Apache-2.0), commit `9d715cc4f5564a990ca8345abfdd5df6dc9b41c8`. Their license texts are retained in `licenses/design-guidance.txt`. Impeccable attribution: https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/9d715cc4f5564a990ca8345abfdd5df6dc9b41c8/skill/reference/document.md.
- The generated wallet bundle retains its linked legal notice in `dist/assets/app.js.LEGAL.txt`.

All runtime images, wallet marks, styles and fonts are local to the static export. There are no external font or image requests in the normal page. Chain reads, oracle reads and an explicit Coinbase wallet connection require their respective public services.
